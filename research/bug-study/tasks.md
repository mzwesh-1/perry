# Perry tasks for the bug study

Each task is short and can be done with the Perry Tutorial. They are chosen so
that the usual money mistakes are easy to make: two currencies, yearly and
monthly rates, percentages, and money read from a file. Do them in order and
save often.

**Task 1 - Shopping list.** A list of prices in rand. Show the total, the total
with 15% VAT, and the most expensive item.

**Task 2 - Travel budget.** A trip costs 12 500.00 ZAR for flights and
850.00 USD for the hotel. The rate is 18.20 ZAR per USD. Show the total in
rand. If the total is more than 30 000.00 ZAR, show "Over budget".

**Task 3 - Home loan.** 950 000.00 ZAR at 11.75% per year over 20 years. Show the
monthly payment and the total interest. Then show how much less interest is
paid at 10.5% per year.

**Task 4 - Savings.** Someone saves 1 500.00 ZAR every month for 3 years at 8% per
year, interest added monthly. Show what they have at the end, and in which
month they first pass 25 000.00 ZAR.

**Task 5 - Bank statement.** Load `statement.csv` (date, description, amount)
as ZAR. Show the total spent, the total received, and every payment over
2 000.00 ZAR. Then remove the zero amounts and show the count.

**Task 6 - Invoices.** Make a blueprint for an invoice (number, client, amount,
paid as yes/no). Make five invoices, one of them in USD. Show the total still
unpaid in rand.

**Task 7 - Mining project.** The project costs 4 000 000.00 USD now and earns
2 500 000.00 ZAR, 3 000 000.00 ZAR and 3 500 000.00 ZAR over the next three years.
The rate is 18.20 ZAR per USD and the cost of capital 12% per year. Show the
NPV and the IRR, and say whether the project is worth doing.

**Task 8 - Salaries.** A team of four shares a bonus pool of 100 000.00 ZAR, split
equally. Each also gets a 6% raise on salaries of your choosing. Show each
person's new monthly salary with the bonus spread over 12 months, and check
that the bonus parts add up to the pool.
